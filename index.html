Why it costs HK$200

280gsm long-staple cotton is roughly 2× the weight of a fast-fashion tee. Garment-dye adds a multi-stage wash process. The whole thing is cut and sewn at one Guangzhou factory on a small batch run. We're not building margin — we're paying for the fabric.

03 The Brand

A single layer, considered.

Ply is a single-ingredient brand. We make heavyweight cotton tees — nothing else. 280gsm and above, garment-dyed, cut to drape clean over a body that's already at rest. Ply is the layer you reach for when the day is unstructured and the plan is to be present in it.

We don't make seasonal drops. We make the same tee, in five colors, in the heaviest weight we can source — and we make it well.
